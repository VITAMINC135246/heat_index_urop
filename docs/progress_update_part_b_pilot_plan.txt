workflow framing has been superseded by the revised A-E project structure in

imagery, visible-image surface-cover information, and LUHK 2024 land-use data,

not the immediate goal.

Current revised workflow
------------------------
A. Data inventory and spatial foundation
   Maintain the V/T pair inventory, metadata summaries, LUHK category
   reference, camera assumptions, and selected HKUST pilot image list. Garden
   Hill images are currently deprioritized for the HKUST-focused workflow.

B. V/T ROI alignment and visible-image surface-cover classification
   Align visible images to thermal images or crop visible images to the thermal
   ROI, classify visible-image surface cover inside the thermal ROI, and record
   alignment/classification QA notes. Manual inspection remains necessary for
   uncertain cases.

C. Temperature extraction and Delta-T table construction
   Extract pixel-level or region-level temperature values from DJI thermal
   images when Windows, DJI Thermal Analysis Tool 3, or DJI Thermal SDK access
   is available. Join temperature, LUHK, surface cover, metadata, and QA fields.

D. Statistical analysis by land-use context and surface cover
   Compare delta-T distributions by LUHK land-use category and visible-image
   surface-cover category.

E. Reporting, visualization, and optional exploratory modeling
   Prepare the final pilot workflow report and figures. Simple exploratory
   models should only be tested after enough reliable labeled data exists.

Classification
--------------
LUHK broad land-use context retains:
Residential; Commercial; Industrial; GIC / open space; Transport; Other urban /
built-up land; Agriculture; Woodland / shrubland / grassland / wetland; Barren
land; Water bodies.

LUHK is a broad-brush 10 m context dataset. A 10 m × 10 m cell is approximately
100 m² and must not be used as drone pixel-level surface-cover ground truth.

Pilot visible-image surface-cover classes:
0 Unknown / excluded
1 Building roof
2 Asphalt
3 Concrete / paved surface
4 Tree canopy
5 Low vegetation
6 Bare soil / rock
7 Water

Shadow is a separate binary mask:
0 not shadowed
1 shadowed

Registration requirement
------------------------
V and T images have different resolutions. One verified pair has a 4032 × 3024
visible image and a 1280 × 1024 thermal JPG, while the target temperature grid
is 640 × 512. Different field of view, sensor position, rotation, scale, and
perspective also mean that simple resizing is not sufficient.

The current LUHK pilot does not assume V and T have identical footprints. It
uses shared LUHK 10 m global_cell_id values to identify cells covered by the
visible footprint, the thermal footprint, or both.

Target mapping:
visible image coordinate → thermal image 640 × 512 coordinate
